Diabetes:
Diabetes is a chronic condition affecting blood sugar levels.
Symptoms include increased thirst, frequent urination, fatigue, blurred vision.
Treatment includes insulin, diet control, exercise.

Hypertension:
High blood pressure increases risk of heart disease and stroke.
Symptoms include headache, dizziness, nosebleeds.
Treatment includes lifestyle changes and medication.

Heart Attack:
A heart attack occurs due to blocked blood flow to the heart.
Symptoms include chest pain, shortness of breath, sweating.
Immediate medical attention is required.

Asthma:
Asthma is a respiratory condition causing breathing difficulty.
Symptoms include wheezing, shortness of breath, chest tightness.
Treatment includes inhalers and avoiding triggers.

Pneumonia:
Infection that inflames air sacs in lungs.
Symptoms include cough, fever, difficulty breathing.
Treatment includes antibiotics and rest.

Tuberculosis:
A bacterial infection affecting lungs.
Symptoms include cough with blood, weight loss, fever.
Treatment requires long-term antibiotics.

Common Cold:
Viral infection affecting nose and throat.
Symptoms include sneezing, runny nose, sore throat.
Treatment includes rest and fluids.

Flu:
A viral infection causing fever, body aches, fatigue.
Treatment includes rest, fluids, antiviral medication.

Migraine:
Severe headache often with nausea and sensitivity to light.
Treatment includes pain relievers and rest.

Gastritis:
Inflammation of stomach lining.
Symptoms include stomach pain, nausea, vomiting.
Treatment includes antacids and diet changes.

Acidity:
Excess stomach acid causing burning sensation.
Treatment includes antacids and avoiding spicy food.

Food Poisoning:
Caused by contaminated food.
Symptoms include vomiting, diarrhea, stomach cramps.
Treatment includes hydration and rest.

Kidney Stones:
Hard deposits in kidneys.
Symptoms include severe pain, blood in urine.
Treatment includes hydration or surgery.

Urinary Tract Infection:
Infection in urinary system.
Symptoms include burning urination, frequent urge.
Treatment includes antibiotics.

Anemia:
Low red blood cells causing weakness.
Symptoms include fatigue, pale skin, dizziness.
Treatment includes iron supplements.

Thyroid Disorder:
Affects metabolism due to hormone imbalance.

Treatment depends on condition.
